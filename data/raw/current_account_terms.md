# Northbridge Bank – Everyday Current Account: Policy Terms

> FICTIONAL DOCUMENT created for a software testing portfolio project. Northbridge Bank does not exist.
> Version 3.2 – effective 1 September 2026
> Product rates, fees and charges are held in the product catalogue, not in this document.

## 1. Who can open an account
You must be 18 or over and a UK resident. You can hold the account on its own or jointly with one other person.

## 2. Payments
Faster Payments up to £25,000 per day can be made through the app or online banking. Standing orders and Direct Debits are free. CHAPS payments must be requested in branch or by phone.

## 3. Closing your account
You can close your account at any time free of charge. If you switch to another bank, we move your regular payments using the Current Account Switch Service. The switch completes in 7 working days.

## 4. Changes to these terms
We will give you at least 2 months' notice before making any change that is to your disadvantage.
