# Northbridge Bank – Arranged Overdraft Guide

> FICTIONAL DOCUMENT created for a software testing portfolio project. Northbridge Bank does not exist.
> Version 2.0 – effective 1 September 2026

## 1. What is an arranged overdraft?
An arranged overdraft is a borrowing limit we agree with you in advance. It lets you keep making payments when your balance falls below £0, up to your agreed limit.

## 2. How much does it cost?
- Overdraft interest rate: 39.9% EAR (variable).
- The first £50 of your arranged overdraft is interest-free.
- There are no fees for using your arranged overdraft. You only pay interest.

### Representative example
If you use an arranged overdraft of £1,200, the interest rate is 39.9% EAR (variable). Representative APR: 39.9% (variable).

## 3. Unarranged overdrafts
If a payment would take you past your arranged limit, or overdrawn without an arranged overdraft, we will normally return the payment unpaid. We do not charge a fee for returned payments.

## 4. Applying for an overdraft
You can apply in the app. We will run a credit check, which leaves a mark on your credit file. Limits range from £100 to £5,000, depending on your circumstances.

## 5. If you are struggling
If you are finding it hard to repay your overdraft, contact our Financial Support Team on 0800 000 0000. We may be able to freeze interest or agree a repayment plan. You can also get free, impartial debt advice from MoneyHelper.

## 6. Overdraft alerts
We send a text alert when you start using your arranged overdraft, and another when you are within £50 of your limit. You cannot switch off these alerts.
