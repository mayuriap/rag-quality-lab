# Northbridge Bank – Complaints, Fraud and Customer Support

> FICTIONAL DOCUMENT created for a software testing portfolio project. Northbridge Bank does not exist.
> Version 1.4 – effective 1 September 2026

## 1. How to make a complaint
You can complain in the app, by phone on 0800 000 0001, or in any branch.
We aim to resolve complaints within 3 working days. If we cannot, we will send you a written response within 8 weeks.

## 2. Financial Ombudsman Service
If you are unhappy with our final response, or we have not responded within 8 weeks, you can refer your complaint to the Financial Ombudsman Service free of charge. You must do this within 6 months of our final response.

## 3. Reporting fraud
If you think someone has accessed your account or you have been tricked into making a payment, call us immediately on 0800 000 0002 (24 hours a day). You can also freeze your card instantly in the app.

## 4. What we will never ask you
Northbridge Bank will never ask you for your full PIN, your full online banking password, or a one-time passcode by phone, text or email. We will never ask you to move money to a "safe account".

## 5. Extra support
If you have a disability, a health condition, or are going through a difficult life event such as bereavement, we can offer extra support, including large-print statements and longer call times. Tell us what you need.

## 6. Opening hours
Phone banking: 8am to 8pm, 7 days a week. Fraud line: 24 hours. Branches: 9am to 5pm, Monday to Saturday.
